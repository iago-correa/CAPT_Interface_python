Speech Comprehensibility Study

Welcome!

Thank you for your participation. Your contribution is essential to our research on computer assisted language learning. Please read the following instructions carefully before you begin.

The Rating Scale (0-9)

You will listen to short audio recordings and rate how easy each one is to understand. Please use the following scale as your guide:

  • 9 - Completely comprehensible: The speech is clear and effortless to understand, like a typical native speaker.
  • 7 - Mostly Comprehensible: The speech is easy to understand, but with very minor problems that don't affect understanding.
  • 5 - Moderately Comprehensible: The speech is generally understandable, but requires some effort from you, the listener.
  • 3 - Barely Comprehensible: You can understand some words or phrases, but with significant effort. The overall meaning is unclear.
  • 0 - Impossible to understand: You cannot understand any of the words being said.
Step-by-Step Guide
  1. Login
    On the next page, please use your designed credential for both the ID and password. You are not supposed to use your Prolific ID, but rather the credentials gave to you by this study.
  2. Select the Part
    After logging in, you will see a 'Selection screen'. Please click on the button Start for the corresponding part.
  3. Rate the Recordings
    Recordings are presented in batches of 10. Listen to each audio file, then select your rating from 0 to 9.
  4. Save and Continue
    After rating all 10 recordings on a page, click the "Next" button to save your ratings and move to the next batch. Your ratings are only saved when you click "Next".
  5. Complete the Study
    After the final batch, you will return to the Part Selection screen. A new link will appear.
    Please remember you need to click "Next" on the final recordings batch to complete all the ratings.
  6. Submit on Prolific
    Click the final link that says "Click here to complete this part on Prolific." to register your completion and receive payment.
